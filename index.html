ADULT CATEGORY

Class 2 Pro-Stock Race Mechanics

Vehicle Class

Class 2 Pro-Stock (1/10 Scale)

The Adult Category is open to 1/10 scale Class 2 Pro-Stock RC crawlers. All vehicles are subject to inspection before racing.

General Specifications

Required

  • Front bumper is required.
  • Manufactured tires measuring 4.30"–4.75".
  • 1.9" wheels only.
  • Maximum wheelbase: 355 mm.
  • Hardbody or Lexan body.
  • No scale points required.
  • Vehicle must pass technical inspection.

Not Allowed

  • No cheater body cut.
  • No DIG.
  • No Four-Wheel Steering (4WS).
  • No switchable Overdrive (OD).

Pro-Stock Chassis Rules

Heavily modified, upgraded and tuned stock rigs are allowed, including RTR competition rigs and custom-built competition rigs, provided they comply with the Class 2 specifications.

  • C-channel or Flat Rails are permitted.
  • Center Skid chassis only.
  • Forward Skid link geometry is not permitted.

Body Requirements

Full Body

  • Coffin-style body shape (Cliffhanger, Tucked, Coyote or similar).

Cab Body

  • No exposed chassis rails.
  • Bed must extend at least between the chassis rails.
  • Bed sides are required.
  • Tailgate is optional.

Additional Requirements

  • No cheater body cut.
  • Tires may extend beyond the front bumper.
  • Chassis (including bumper) must be approximately 3 mm wider than the body shell.

Vehicle Inspection

Every vehicle will undergo inspection before racing. Vehicles that fail inspection must be corrected before being allowed to compete.

KIDS CATEGORY

Kids Category Race Mechanics

Age Requirements

4 years old to 12 years old.

Vehicle Requirements

  • Open to 1/10 and 1/12 scale RC crawlers.
  • Depending on the number of participants, racers may be grouped by scale: 1/10 versus 1/10 and 1/12 versus 1/12. However, if there are insufficient participants in either scale, the organiser may combine both scales into a single race bracket.
  • The vehicle must be safe to operate and pass technical inspection before racing.

Race Format

  • The competition follows a 1 vs 1 elimination format.
  • Racers will be paired through a random draw before the start of the event.
  • Each match consists of two racers running simultaneously on identical courses.

Winning a Match

The winner of each match is determined by:

  1. Successfully completing the course.
  2. If both racers finish, the racer with the faster completion time advances.
  3. If only one racer finishes within the time limit, that racer advances automatically.
  4. If neither racer finishes, the racer who progresses farther through the course advances.

Tournament Progression

  • Winners advance to the next elimination round.
  • The tournament continues until only one racer remains.
  • Depending on the number of participants, the organiser may conduct quarterfinals, semifinals, and finals.

Time Limit

Each match has a maximum time limit of 10 minutes.

Penalties

  • Boundary violations
  • Unsportsmanlike conduct
  • Outside assistance

Marshals' Decision

  • Course marshals monitor every run.
  • The marshal's decision regarding penalties, progress, and finish order is final.

Awards

  • Champion
  • 1st Runner-up
  • 2nd Runner-up

Tie-breaker

If two racers record exactly the same finishing time, the two racers will restart the race.
