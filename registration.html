Kids Category

Kids Add-on — ₱100 per child

Includes one Kids Category race entry only. No food, event shirt, or raffle entry.

Kids may also use Early or Late full-registration packages. Kids walk-in registration is ₱100.

Registration Rules

  • One registration is valid for one RC rig only.
  • An Adult Category rig may also be used by one child with a valid Kids Category registration.
  • An Adult Category rig may only be shared once with a single Kids Category participant.
  • An Adult Category rig cannot be shared by two or more adult participants.
  • All rigs are subject to technical inspection before racing.

Payment

Pay through GCash and enter the payment reference number in the form.

Registration fees are non-refundable but may be transferred to another participant with prior organizer approval.
